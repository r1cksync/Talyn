2. Product workflow

Build a multi-tenant platform with this complete workflow:

* A hiring manager registers, verifies their email, and creates an organization.
* The manager creates a job with its description, seniority, required skills, interview duration, and evaluation criteria.
* The manager adds shortlisted candidates individually or through CSV import.
* Each candidate can have a resume and optional cover letter uploaded as PDF or DOCX.
* The system extracts relevant information, generates a personalized interview plan using LangGraph and Amazon Bedrock, and identifies extraction problems.
* The manager can preview and edit questions and the scoring rubric.
* When the manager launches the interview campaign, the platform automatically emails invitations to the selected candidates.
* Candidates open a secure invitation link, verify access, review disclosures, grant microphone/camera permissions, complete a device check, and start their interview.
* An AI interviewer asks questions aloud and displays them as text.
* Candidates answer verbally. The platform captures audio, produces live transcripts, and records video according to the disclosed recording policy.
* The AI asks relevant follow-up questions within defined time and difficulty limits.
* After the interview, the platform generates separate manager and candidate reports and automatically emails each recipient their appropriate report notification.
* Managers review supporting evidence and make hiring decisions themselves.

Include a fully working synthetic demonstration of this workflow.

7. LangGraph workflows

Implement actual typed LangGraph graphs with persistent PostgreSQL checkpoints, versioned state, retry policies, bounded execution, and observable transitions.

Use separate logical workflows for:

A. Interview preparation

Document ingestion → structured extraction → job competency mapping → candidate evidence mapping → question generation → rubric alignment → quality checks → interview plan persistence.

Generate:

* Core questions shared across candidates for the same job.
* Personalized questions grounded in the candidate’s relevant experience.
* Technical, behavioral, and situational questions where appropriate.
* Expected evidence and scoring anchors for each question.
* Follow-up rules and time limits.

Use personalization to explore relevant claims without silently changing the job’s evaluation standard.

B. Interview execution

Session validation → question selection → question delivery → answer collection → answer completeness assessment → optional follow-up → progress update → next question or completion.

Bound follow-ups, total duration, token usage, and repeated clarification loops. Persist enough state to recover after process restarts.

Do not allow the interview model to execute arbitrary code, browse arbitrary URLs, change authorization, or invoke administrative tools.

C. Evaluation and reporting

Finalize transcript → associate answers with questions → assess rubric dimensions → verify evidence references → assess completeness → generate separate reports → enqueue email notifications.

All structured model outputs must pass schema validation. Handle invalid JSON, timeouts, throttling, and unavailable models explicitly.

Keep side effects such as sending emails outside replayable LLM nodes or protect them with durable idempotency controls. Resuming a graph must not blindly resend invitations.

9. Video recording and review observations

Capture video with clear candidate consent and a visible recording indicator.

Implement incremental recording upload to private S3 storage with bounded browser memory, retry handling, sequence numbers, checksums, timestamps, and a final manifest.

Verify browser codec support. Do not assume independently generated media chunks can simply be concatenated into a valid recording. Implement and test the chosen assembly or playback strategy.

For the MVP, analyze sampled frames at a configurable, cost-bounded rate using appropriate Amazon Rekognition APIs. Store neutral observations such as:

* No face detected.
* Multiple faces detected.
* Camera unavailable or obscured.
* Estimated head orientation away from the camera.
* Estimated eye direction, where supported.
* Significant frame changes requiring manual review.

Distinguish face count from person count. If detecting additional people is implemented, use an appropriate person-detection capability and report its limitations.

Store timestamps, confidence, duration, and limited supporting evidence. Use temporal smoothing and minimum-duration thresholds to reduce transient alerts.

Never label gaze, posture, movement, or a missing face as proof of cheating. Do not calculate a cheating probability, deduct interview marks from these signals, or automatically reject candidates.

Do not infer personality, honesty, emotion, disability, ethnicity, or other sensitive attributes from appearance or voice. Do not build face identification or biometric matching for the MVP.

Keep video observations separate from competency evaluations. Provide a human review timeline, a way to dismiss observations, candidate explanations, and an accommodation path.

Clearly document that webcam monitoring cannot establish whether a candidate used off-camera assistance.

* Any missing or contradictory evidence.
* An indication when evidence is insufficient.

Distinguish unanswered questions, technical failures, low transcription quality, and incorrect answers. Do not turn missing or unreliable audio into a confident negative assessment.

Do not score appearance, eye contact, accent, or inferred personal characteristics. Evaluate communication only through explicit, job-relevant criteria supported by answer content.

Manager report:

* Interview summary.
* Competency scores and evidence.
* Strengths and areas needing further assessment.
* Incomplete or technically affected sections.
* Separately displayed video observations.

Candidate report:

* Questions or competencies covered.
* Constructive feedback grounded in their answers.
* Strengths and practical improvement suggestions.
